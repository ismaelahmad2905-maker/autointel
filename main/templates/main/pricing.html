Pricing & Transparency

AutoIntel provides estimated part guidance only. Prices shown in the platform should be treated as rough reference values, not fixed quotations.

Price Disclaimer

Car part prices can vary significantly depending on the vehicle make, model, year, supplier, region, labour costs, stock availability, and whether parts are original, aftermarket, or refurbished.

For that reason, any prices shown by AutoIntel are intended for educational and planning purposes only. Users should always confirm final pricing with a qualified mechanic, dealership, or parts supplier before purchasing.

What Affects Cost?

  • Vehicle brand and trim level
  • Part availability and supplier pricing
  • OEM vs aftermarket parts
  • Labour rates by workshop and region
  • Import, delivery, and urgent repair costs

Ethical Considerations

AutoIntel is designed to assist users, not replace professional judgment. AI-generated suggestions may be useful for early guidance, but they should not be treated as guaranteed diagnoses or final repair decisions.

  • Users should be aware that AI predictions can be wrong or incomplete.
  • Safety-critical vehicle issues should always be checked by a qualified mechanic.
  • Price estimates should not be used to mislead customers or misrepresent true repair costs.
  • Mechanics and users should apply human judgment before acting on recommendations.
  • The platform should be used responsibly, transparently, and with consideration for fairness and trust.
